Eviction case processing, made visible.

Alloy One pairs eviction counsel with software built for the work — so property management companies can see where every case stands, what it will cost, and what needs to happen next.

What we're building

Case visibility

A real-time view of where each case sits in the legal process — instead of emailing the firm and waiting.

Predictable cost

Flat-fee legal work with cost-to-date, forecast resolution, and a confidence rating that tightens as a case moves.

Bulk notice generation

Statutory notices drafted from delinquency data, with amounts auto-totaled — the math errors that get cases thrown out, removed.

Action prompts

The platform says when you need to act to keep a case moving, and tells the firm side what each case needs next.

How it works

One platform, both sides

Property managers create notices, submit intakes, and track progress. Attorneys and paralegals receive intakes, assemble and file documents, coordinate service, and move cases to resolution — with a deliberate line between what's shared and what stays firm-internal.

Built for the jurisdiction

Deadlines, notice periods, service rules, and documents differ by state and county as a matter of statute. Alloy One treats that as law, not localization. Launching in Washington State, with additional states to follow.
